Our trading expertise spans a broad spectrum across Fertilizers, Technical Grade Urea, Sulphur, Gypsum, Ferrous & Non-Ferrous Metals, Precious Metals, Steel, and Industrial Equipment.

Technical Grade Urea (46% Nitrogen) ⌄
High-purity industrial urea with consistent 46% nitrogen content, low biuret levels, and high solubility—used in resins, adhesives, melamine, diesel exhaust fluid (AdBlue/AUS 32), pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, and textile finishing.
Fertilizers & Agricultural Inputs ⌄
Ammonium Sulphate, Ammonium Nitrate, Calcium Ammonium Nitrate (CAN), Potassium Chloride (MOP), Potassium Sulphate (SOP), Single Super Phosphate (SSP), Diammonium Phosphate (DAP), and Monoammonium Phosphate (MAP).
Sulphur, Natural Gypsum, Soda Ash & Micronutrients ⌄
Granular, Powdered, and Lump Sulphur, Natural Gypsum, Soda Ash, Intermediate Chemicals, and custom-ratio NPK Compound Fertilizers sourced from premier global producers.
Non-Ferrous & Base Metals ⌄
Copper (Cathodes, Wire Rods, Scrap), Aluminum (Ingots & Allied Products), Zinc (Ingots), and Lead (Battery Scrap) delivered through reliable maritime routes.
Ferrous Metals & Structural Steel ⌄
Iron Ore (Lumps, Fines, Pellets), Hot Briquetted Iron (HBI), Steel Plates, Coils, Sheets, ERW/Seamless/Galvanized Pipes & Tubes, Beams, Channels, Angles, TMT Bars, and Wire Rods.
Physical Precious Metals (Gold & Silver) ⌄
Physical Gold and Silver trading backed by transparent provenance, strict compliance, and secure international settlement.
Heavy Industrial Equipment & Windmill Accessories (Sourcing) ⌄
Dedicated global sourcing for Mobile Cranes, Excavators, Loaders, Dozers, Forklifts & Reach Stackers, as well as Windmill Turbines, Blades, Nacelles, Hubs, and Tower Sections.
