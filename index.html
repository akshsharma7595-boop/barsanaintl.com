01 // Institutional Profile

Built on Physical Supply Reliability & Maritime Discipline.

Barsana International LLC operates from the United Arab Emirates as a multi-commodity trading, import-export, and maritime logistics enterprise. In a volatile global supply environment, industrial buyers require more than brokerage—they require assured physical allocation, strict adherence to chemical and metallurgical specifications, and synchronized vessel chartering.

Through long-standing off-take relationships with primary producers in the Middle East, Central Asia, and the Indian Ocean Rim, we manage the complete transaction lifecycle: from mill and mine origin inspection to bulk vessel fixture, port stevedoring, and documentary credit execution.

03 // End-to-End Execution

Maritime Logistics & Chartering Workflow

From producer stockyard to discharge port, Barsana International coordinates every operational milestone with precision.

STAGE 01

Origin Allocation & Mill Audit

We lock in physical tonnage directly with verified refineries, chemical plants, and steel mills, validating batch production schedules and export readiness.

STAGE 02

Independent Pre-Shipment Assay

Accredited surveyors (SGS / Bureau Veritas / Intertek) conduct sampling, chemical analysis, weight verification, and draft surveys at load port.

STAGE 03

Vessel Chartering & Stevedoring

Our logistics desk charters dry-bulk vessels, breakbulk coasters, or container blocks, managing port berth scheduling and weather-safe loading.

STAGE 04

Documentary Clearance & Delivery

Prompt presentation of clean Bills of Lading, Certificates of Origin, and Phytosanitary/Assay certificates for smooth customs clearance at destination.

Standard Trading Procedures & FAQs

For containerized industrial chemicals, fertilizers, and non-ferrous metals, our typical minimum lot starts at 500 MT (20x 20' FCL). For dry-bulk commodities such as Natural Gypsum, Sulphur, Bulk Urea, and Iron Ore, we handle vessel parcels ranging from 5,000 MT to 50,000+ MT Supramax cargoes.
Every contract includes mandatory independent inspection at the port of loading by SGS, Bureau Veritas, or Intertek. Certificate of Quality (CoQ) and Certificate of Weight (CoW) issued at load port form an integral part of our shipping documentation.
